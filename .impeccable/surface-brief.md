# Dashboard direction

MODE: Operate. A student checks a month, records a transaction and adjusts a plan.
FORM: Campus botanical wayfinding; seed:33820fcbd2336374, assigned candidate 7. User delegated execution, so no further choice round is held.
Grounded candidates: campus library typography, Japanese stationery, student union notices, cooperative grocery signage, university prospectus, campus cafe menus, campus botanical wayfinding.
World contribution stays limited to typography, green/warm palette, readable density and the budget track. Standard app navigation, forms and tables remain familiar.
LAYOUT: Sidebar selects dataset and task. Header selects month. Four summary values share one band. Two unequal columns contain history and savings; category chart and recent transactions follow.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Product name, unmistakable synthetic-data label, selected month, income/expense/surplus and the upper history plot must be visible at desktop size.
SIGNATURE: A straight green budget/goal track places recorded spending or surplus against the student's own limit. Numbers and explanatory text remain present when the track saturates.
TYPE: Bundled Source Serif Pro headings and bundled Streamlit sans body; tabular monetary values.
COLOR: Pale warm paper with sage navigation, forest green income and burnt orange expenses. Red/amber reserved for invalid or over-budget states.
MOTION: Native control feedback only; no entrance animation or decorative motion.
CONTENT: Indonesian interface, integer JPY, six synthetic months, isolated demo/private SQLite databases. Savings metric explicitly means monthly surplus, not actual bank savings.
CONSTRAINTS: Local-only server, no credential entry, no paid services, no bank integration. No user finances are shipped in Git.
