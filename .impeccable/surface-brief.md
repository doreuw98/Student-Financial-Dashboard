# Dashboard direction

MODE: Operate. A student reviews a month, records transactions and adjusts a plan.
FORM: User-pinned Purple reference. Light lilac workspace, white sidebar and panels, slim white account bar and bright summary cards.
LAYOUT: Sidebar selects the dataset and task. Month stays by the page heading. Income, expenses and monthly balance have equal cards. A wider trend panel and narrower category panel sit above recent records and goal progress.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Keep the brand, selected month, active dataset and three summary amounts clear. Bring the chart headings directly below the metrics.
SIGNATURE: Pink, blue and teal gradient summary cards with a clipped translucent circle. Charts use purple/pink grouped trend bars and a multicolor category donut.
TYPE: Source Sans Pro with Japanese Gothic and generic sans fallbacks. Use tabular amounts and compact headings.
COLOR: Lilac canvas, white panels, purple controls and deep plum text. Status feedback uses native alert colors.
MOTION: Native control feedback only.
CONTENT: Japanese-first with English and Indonesian options. Comma-grouped integer JPY, localized periods, six synthetic months and isolated per-account demo/private SQLite databases. Monthly balance is income minus expenses. Goal progress includes numeric values and explanatory text.
ACCOUNT SURFACES: White welcome panel beside native login/register tabs, with compact padding and reduced supplementary copy on mobile. Sidebar includes account name, settings and sign-out. Settings uses a native language selector and explicit save.
CONSTRAINTS: Local server and local accounts. Keep financial records and account data outside Git.
